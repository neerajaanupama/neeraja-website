01 A little about me

I’m Neeraja Anupama.

Anupama is inspired by the idea of being incomparable — not by being better than anyone else, but by being unmistakably yourself.

This space brings together the things that shape me, the things I care about, and the journey I’m building along the way.

One part of that journey is dance: a space for expression, learning, and sharing what I love with others.

Anupama is a reflection of who I am today and an idea I hope to grow into something meaningful.

One name. One story. Always becoming.
