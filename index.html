A little about me

I grew up between languages and landscapes — Kerala’s coastline, Delhi’s poetry evenings, Bengaluru’s classrooms — and somewhere along the way, storytelling tied them all together.

I’m a communications professional with three years across content, design and digital strategy. My work lives where words, images and impact meet — turning the quiet, everyday work of social-change organisations into stories people actually feel.

At Makkala Jagriti, I write the newsletters and blogs, design the reports and learning resources, script and edit the films, and hold the brand together across every channel and event. Off the clock, I’m a literature graduate, a published author, a dancer, and an incurable flâneur — happiest wandering a city, collecting small moments to write about later.
