a letter, for your birthday

You are Anupama —

incomparable, and I mean it
the way the name was made.

No one has ever come into my life
the way you did:

softly, completely,
like morning light through leaves.

Beautiful in the face — yes —

but a beautiful soul first,
and that is the rarer thing.

Kind. Luminous. Tender. Blooming.

Entirely your own.

incomparable
